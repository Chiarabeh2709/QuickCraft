1. What QuickCraft is

QuickCraft is a website where customers describe a job and tradespeople send quotes for it. We bring the two sides together. We do not do the work, we do not employ the tradespeople, and we are not part of the agreement between a customer and a tradesperson. Payments do not go through QuickCraft.

2. Demonstration service

QuickCraft is a demonstration project made for a university course. Please do not use it to arrange real work and do not enter real personal data. Data can be deleted at any time, for example when the service restarts.

3. Your account

  • Give correct information and keep it up to date. One person has one account.
  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens with your account.
  • Tradespeople give their company name, trade, service area and a qualification reference number. An administrator checks the details before a tradesperson can see jobs and send quotes, and approves each additional trade separately. We can reject an account or a trade, or remove an account that breaks these terms.

4. Customers

  • Post only jobs you really want done, with a true description.
  • When you accept a quote and confirm an appointment, this is meant as an agreement between you and that tradesperson for that price and that time. Please keep the appointment or cancel it in good time.

5. Tradespeople

  • Send only quotes you can keep: a true price, a true earliest start date, and the time the service really takes. You can change a quote only until the customer decides on it.
  • Keep your weekly availability correct, and keep the appointments you were given.
  • Offer only trades you are qualified for. Licences, insurance, taxes and safety rules are your own responsibility.

6. Contact details and your data

  • We store your name, email, phone number and address, and for tradespeople the company name, trades, service area and qualification reference number. We also store your messages and reviews, and when you accepted these terms. We do not ask for identity documents and do not store uploaded documents.
  • A customer's phone number and address are shown to a tradesperson only after the customer accepted that tradesperson's quote or booked their slot. A customer can also choose to share a phone number in a conversation, and can stop sharing it. A tradesperson's phone number and address never appear on a public profile or in search.
  • Use contact details only for the job they were shared for. Do not pass them on, copy them or use them for advertising.
  • You can ask for your data to be shown, corrected or deleted, as the data protection law (GDPR) allows.

7. Messages and reviews

  • Be polite. No insults, threats or harassment.
  • A review is for a job that was done and only from the customer of that job, once, for the tradesperson who did the work. Write it honestly. We can remove reviews and messages that break these terms.

8. What is not allowed

  • False information, fake quotes, fake jobs or fake reviews.
  • Using the site to do anything illegal, or to collect other people's data.
  • Disturbing the service, for example by automated mass requests.

9. Availability and liability

We try to keep the site running, but we do not promise that it is always available. We do not guarantee the quality, price or punctuality of any work, and we are not responsible for damage that happens between a customer and a tradesperson. Rights that the law gives you and cannot be excluded stay as they are.

10. Changes

If we change these terms, the version above changes, and you have to accept the new version before you can continue to use your account.
